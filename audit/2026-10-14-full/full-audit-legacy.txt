1. STATIC RANK EVENT REALLY USES ONE COMMON PROFILE SPACE

of n uniform strict preference permutations. activeRank is the ONE-INDEXED
rank of the first currently available good, not its index among survivors.
activeRank_tail_probability identifies its law with
choose(n-r, |S|)/choose(n, |S|). UniformSubsetRank proves the exponential tail
using the without-replacement subset count, including the zero-numerator
case. There is no replacement of the preference model by independent ranks
for different active sets.

RankMGF.mgf_scaled_rank_le_one_add_exp telescopes exp(cR) with the indicator
r<R, r=0,...,n-1. Thus the one-indexing and terminal rank n are retained.
The MGF at half the scaled rank is bounded by 1+e. UniformProductChernoff
factorizes over AGENTS in the genuine product sample, then unions over
at most 2^n active sets on that same sample. This gives
UniformPermutation.uniform_active_rank_exponential_profile.

PSProcess.uniformRankGood_failure_probability_le uses its exact failure
complement. ConcreteTheoremOne.rankConstant =
2*(log 2+1+log(1+e)) leaves rate exactly one, giving exp(-n).
PSProcess.run_rankReduction combines the static event with the dynamic-rank
identity for the actual allocation, yielding
averageRank <= rankConstant * eatingIntegral.
The algebra retains the n/|S| factor and divides aggregate rank by n once.
All active phases have a nonempty active set and positive duration.

2. ADMISSIBILITY AND TREE LAW ARE DISCHARGED ON ACTUAL PS PATHS

PSChoiceMachine.queryAvailable is the active set at a fresh query; it is the
singleton containing the previous choice at a persistent query; after

profile_machine_correspondence identifies the labelled machine's supply and

profileRevealHistory_admissible establishes the complete trace condition.

SequentialRevelation.sequential_revelation_joint_count proves exact profile
counts for every admissible finite reveal extension. The swap bijection keeps
all previous answers fixed and interchanges any two currently available goods;
it does not assume the endogenous active set independent of preferences.

PSChoiceMachine.pathFiber_card_mul_revealProduct_le uses actual history fibers
as subsets of compatible reveal profiles, and

inverse product of reveal-set sizes. Thus
pathFiber_probability_le_pathWeight is a proved domination statement. It does

3. STOPPED COUNTS, PROPORTIONAL COMPENSATORS, AND PHASE FAILURES

setRecording records only fresh entries while all goods of B are still active.
On a genuine nested trace it cannot resume after B loses a good.
successes_at_completed_round proves the recorded count equals phaseLoad B
for every B contained in the active set at that phase. Old consumers persist
in B and genuine incoming consumers supply precisely the increment.
compensator_card_proportional proves predictable volumes of any two surviving
sets proportional to their cardinalities; no conditional independent-choice
claim is used in this deterministic identity.

In treeFailure_of_phase_bad, the completed round is at depth (round+1)*n.
run_phase_profileSupply_index proves round<n, so this depth is <=n*n, including
the last possible phase. If the active-set volume is <=eta*n, its success
count is n, giving the potential-failure event. Otherwise, a subset B of the
underloaded goods with the exact candidate size is chosen. Proportionality
provides volume_B>=eta*|B|*lambda, and underload gives the required strict
success-count inequality. This realizes the fixed-set underload event. The
random active/B choices are covered by the tree's unions over fixed sets.
The lambda used for irrelevant small inverse-cardinality states is enlarged
to max(phaseLambdaZero,n/s), ensuring all abstract threshold assumptions; at
phases relevant to the bad event it equals n/s exactly.

AdaptiveCategorical.hitProbability includes both the present node and every
future prefix through the specified finite depth. Its exponential-process
maximal inequality is proved by finite tree induction, with positive threshold
and normalized nonnegative law. StoppedChoices instantiates it for arbitrary
predictable recording rules. Upper and lower deviation signs and the
lowerRate=1/2-(log 2)/4 coefficient agree with their process exponents.

The bad event need not persist. PSChoiceMachine.profile_hit_probability_le_
hitProbability handles this correctly by introducing pastEvent, which checks
all suffixes of the newest-first history, proving persistence, and proving its
hitting probability from [] equals the original event's hitting probability.
The drop identity recovers every actual earlier prefix including depth zero
and the terminal depth. This avoids losing a violation that subsequently
ceases to hold.

PSChoiceMachine.concrete_load_transfer explicitly composes actual load failure
=> tree hit, the genuine profile/tree domination, and load_bound_tree_failure.
Its conclusion is exp(-(I(etaZero)-log 2)*n)+n^(-3), with a proved positive

5. FINAL QUANTIFIERS AND MANUSCRIPT SCOPE

PaperTheoremOne.paper_tail_bound holds for positive n with log n>=1 and retains
all three terms exp(-n)+exp(-loadRate*n)+n^(-3). StandardLogStar compares the
legacy phase counter to the actual least natural-log iteration count; the
factor two is an explicit relaxation, not a redefinition of log-star.

failureError_le_paper_tail_eventually chooses paperRate=min(1,loadRate)/2,
then uses log 2<=paperRate*n to absorb BOTH exponentials. Its polynomial
constant is >=1. paper_tail_displayed_form is explicitly EVENTUAL and does not
claim its fixed displayed constants work for all positive market sizes.

The added paper_tail_all_positive genuinely removes that restriction:
first take one eventual threshold N, choose C=max(paperConstant,N), then
quantify over n. Thus C and c are absolute, chosen before n. For n<N, rank<=n
makes the tail event empty because C*(1+logStar n)>=n. For n>=N, the enlarged
rank threshold and polynomial constant preserve the eventual inequality.
This proves the same tail form for every positive ONE-COPY market size.

The RP denominator in expectedPS_div_actual_RP_tendsto_zero is the expectation
of the actual one-copy RP process, connected by rpExpectedRank_eq_knuth_formula;
it is not an unrelated quantity merely named RP. Full replica RP/PS comparisons
remain a different, unproved claim, as the status documentation says.

The revised paper's Theorem 1 is quota-uniform. This legacy proof gives its
q=1 high-probability special case only. Moreover, it uses the older static
active-rank/stopped-choice proof, rather than formalizing the revised paper's
common raw-draw sampling argument verbatim. These are scope distinctions,
not a counterexample to the proved one-copy theorem. Current
THEOREM1_STATUS.md already correctly marks the quota-uniform extension open.


6. INDEPENDENT LOAD/PHASE CROSS-CHECK

Independent legacy phase/load audit, 2026-10-14
Repository comparison point: e0b0f4f
Files: LoadBound.lean, PhaseIntegral.lean, PhaseAdapter.lean
Read-only audit; no build or source modification.

Concrete defects found: none.

Scope assurance:
1. The load union is over fixed candidate subsets and finite-tree hitting
   events, so its probability already controls all histories/times up to the
   depth. There is no missing additional union over arrival time in these
   modules. The actual PS law transfer is reviewed separately by theorem_two.
2. The fixed-B lower-tail estimate pays beta*|B|*lambda. The entropy bound
   choose(n,b)<=(e*n/b)^b and b>=n*exp(-alpha*lambda) consume at most half the
   exponent when 1+alpha*lambda <= (beta/2)*lambda. The remaining half is at
   least 4*log(n) because beta=8*alpha and b*lambda>=log(n)/alpha. Thus each
   cardinality costs n^-4 and summing over n positive cardinalities costs n^-3.
   The separate potential union pays 2^n and leaves exp(-(I(eta)-log2)*n).
   eta=1/16 guarantees that this exponential has a positive rate.
3. The b*lambda lower bound handles both ceiling regimes correctly. For
   base=n*exp(-alpha*lambda)<1, b>=1 and alpha*lambda>log n. For base>=1,
   1<=alpha*lambda<=log n and monotonicity of y*exp(-y) on [1,infinity)
   supplies the bound. The assumptions log(n)>=1 and lambda>=1/alpha remain
   explicit; no small-n claim is silently introduced here.
4. Ceiling rounding is controlled correctly: ceil(base)<base+1; for base>=1,
   ceil(base)<=2*base. This gives the factor four in the active-ratio recurrence.
   For base<=1, the next active cardinality is at most two. Equality base=1 is
   valid in both statements. The threshold 2*ceil(base)<active for active>=3
   follows from 2*lambda*exp(-alpha*lambda)<=1/4; at active=3 the strict margin
   is still positive. No integer boundary is lost.
5. The initial block is bounded by lambdaZero times total duration one.
   Every raw middle block is a sublist of the actual run and chooses the last
   phase's active set as its fixed survivor set. Nested active sets keep that
   survivor available in every phase of its block. The candidate size decreases
   along the block, so the last active set is large enough for each phase's load
   estimate. Consumption from this fixed survivor set is at most its cardinality,
   yielding the 8/eta bound after cancellation. Simultaneous exhaustion events
   and blocks containing only one phase satisfy the same construction.
6. Greedy decomposition covers the phase list exactly, without duplication or
   omitted terminal phases. The terminal suffix is contained in a carrier of
   at most two goods. Supply conservation proves its duration <=2/n, so its
   eating integral is <=2. The small-active-cardinality argument is not merely
   assumed as a time estimate.
7. Consecutive nonterminal raw blocks satisfy lambdaNext>=exp(alpha*lambda)/4.
   The absolute starting-scale condition converts this to uNext>=exp(u) for
   u=alpha*lambda/2. Every block that has a following middle block starts below
   log(n)/2; otherwise its next active set would already be terminal. The count
   applies to blocks.length-1 and then adds one for the final block. This is
   the correct off-by-one allowance, including zero- and one-block cases.
8. The exponential-height definition is total and its chain-count proof does
   not assume a nonexistent infinite phase sequence. The final local bound is
   in terms of PhaseAdapter.psLogStar, a fixed shifted exponential-height
   normalization. The separate StandardLogStar bridge and final public wrapper
   need to identify this with the paper's log-star normalization; that bridge
   is outside these three modules' responsibility.

No defect requiring a repair was found in the load/phase assembly itself.
