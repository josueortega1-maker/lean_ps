RESULT
No concrete false statement, undischarged process hypothesis, off-by-one error,
or numerical-certificate defect was found in the audited upper-bound chain.
No source edit is justified by this audit. The public finite +3.4 and below-five
theorems genuinely concern expected original type rank in the actual integer-
quota replica model. This is a source/semantic audit plus an independent exact-
rational certificate check; the parent agent owns the fresh all-project kernel
build and axiom audit. I did not duplicate that full build.

1. CONDITIONAL LAW AND WEIGHTED EXPONENTIAL PROCESS

ReplicaChoiceMachine.pathFiber_eq_compatibleProfiles identifies the complete
copy-history fiber with compatibility with the fresh TYPE observations. The
reverse direction, history_eq_of_compatible, reconstructs fresh answers from
observations, persistent answers from previousType, copy labels from the
representative function, and the empty-state answer from defaultGood. Thus the
law transfer does not silently discard preference information revealed by copy
changes.

ReplicaHistory.fresh_query_admissible proves, on the generated trace, the
nested-available-set/excluded-previous-answer condition required by the swap
argument. ReplicaRevelation.answerFiber_card_mul_available then supplies exact
uniformity over currently available types. ReplicaLaw.fresh_answer_probability
uses the nonempty actual fiber; its denominators are proved positive.

ReplicaLaw.machineWeight is the exact empirical conditional law on reachable
histories and a harmless deterministic extension elsewhere.
ReplicaLaw.machineWeight_valid proves positivity and normalization.
ReplicaLaw.profile_supermartingale_expectation_le proves the expectation
transfer by actual profile fibers, rather than assuming independent repeated
choices or invoking the one-copy uniform-copy law.

WeightedArrival.weightedArrivalProcess_supermartingale is a finite-horizon
Bernoulli conditional-expectation calculation. Predictability is encoded by
weights/functions of the preceding history. Its stronger allowance of arbitrary
real theta/weights does not break the proof: the exact one-step expression is
bounded by exp(x) >= 1+x. ReplicaLaw.
profile_weightedArrivalProcess_expectation_le_one instantiates this on the
actual profile law. No optional-stopping or infinite-limit premise is hidden.

2. EXPOSURE, CAPACITY, SURVIVORS, AND NORMALIZATION

ReplicaChoiceMachine.weightedCompensator_eq_exposure and
weightedSuccesses_eq_consumption are proved for a type surviving whenever its
horizon weight is positive. Nonfresh within-type copy transitions have zero
entry contribution. The n*n slots (n=m*q) include the complete finite copy run;
extra padded/repeated slots have zero fresh contribution. The initial n choices
are included. At t=1 the terminal ramp vanishes by roundTime_terminal=1.

ReplicaCapacity.residual_nonneg, residual_le_quota, sum_residual,
residual_pos_implies_active and exists_last_survivor supply the needed capacity
and survivor facts from the concrete run. In
expected_profileExposureCost_le, S consists of types with strictly positive
residual. The proof shows its residual sum is m*q*(1-t), so it is nonempty for
0<t<1. This includes all and only the positive residual mass, with no unsupported
conditioning on a random survivor set.

FiniteSurvivor.survivor_log_envelope uses the pathwise Jensen inequality over S
and the average of positive exponentials over ALL m types. Its expected version
uses positivity and E[(sum M)/m] <= 1, then log y <= y-1. It never assumes S
independent of arrivals. The endpoint lemma requires only one last surviving
type; expected_profileExposureCost_endpoint_le supplies it by
exists_last_survivor. This gives the genuine endpoint bound
E profileExposureCost(theta,1) <= q*theta+log m.

rawExposure weights are profile probability times the predictable 1/a weight;
actualExposure divides every weight by q. Their value/cost identities are exact
finite sums. Thus actualExposure_cost_le has the correctly normalized interior
bound phi(theta,1-t), using phi_quota_reduction for q>=1, and
actualExposure_cost_endpoint_quota_le retains theta+log(m)/q. The weaker endpoint
wrapper theta+log m follows legitimately from q>=1 and m>=1.

FiniteExposure.objective_le_phi/phi_attained correctly maximize
-log(a)+lambda*(1-u/a) over u<=a<=1. The three branches meet at lambda=1 and
lambda=1/u. phi_div_antitone and phi_quota_reduction use only their explicitly
proved positivity/domain premises. No maximization over inadmissible a is used.

3. EXPECTED RANK BRIDGE

ReplicaExpectedRank.expected_rank_jump_fiber_le_refined uses the actual history
fiber, the conditional rank-jump theorem, and the fact that a genuine switch
has fewer than m types remaining. It proves the post-initial factor m-1.
expected_weightedRankJump_le_affine treats the initial rank-one observation
separately, adding 1/m once per agent, only at round zero.
expectedPS_le_of_affine_jumps sums that correction to B after division by n;
it does not add it once per round or multiply it by quota.

The resulting expectedPS_le_actualExposure_affine is exactly
E Rank <= 1+(1-1/m)*(value(actualExposure,1)-1).
The weaker expectedPS_le_actualExposure used in PaperReplicaInterface is also
an unconditional consequence. No rank/exposure assumption remains in the
public theorems.

4. FINITE ANALYTIC CLOSURE AND PHASE COUNT

FiniteAtomicExposure.Data has nonnegative finite weights and nonnegative atom
times. Its value is a sum of ramps and cost a sum of exponential arrival costs.
truncated_cost and increment_cost prove the increment estimate atom by atom;
no unproved exchange of expectation and integration is needed.

FinitePhaseGrowth uses precisely the paper's z_j=(5/6)2^(-j),
c_j=exp(z_j)-1-z_j, x_0=e and
x_(j+1)=(c_(j+1)/c_j)*e*z_j*x_j*exp(x_j).
It proves x_j>=e*2^j, x_(j+1)>=exp(x_j), and the tower comparison.
The stoppingIndex is the first j with x_j>=log m. stopping_phase_count proves
stoppingIndex(m)+1 <= logStar(m)-1 for m>=3. The left side includes the terminal
interval, so there is no missing +1. The subtraction on the right is justified
by logStar_ge_two. All queried phase horizons are strictly between zero and one.

FiniteAtomicExposure.logarithmic_bound supplies both interior phase and terminal
estimates to FinitePhaseSum.phase_assembly. The identities
phi_at_next_phase and unit_phase_cost yield exp(z_j) for each contribution.
The initial envelope is <2.108 and the finite excess sum is <2.291. The assembly
therefore yields a margin of 3.399, loosened to 17/5=3.4 in the public conclusion.
The alternate finite geometric-series proof of the excess bound is valid even
though the manuscript writes an infinite-series expression.

FinitePhaseConstants proves its exponential and logarithmic enclosures with
rational Taylor bounds, not floating-point evaluation. Positivity of denominators
and the initial-time interval .82796<s0<.82797 are explicit. Small markets are
covered separately by PaperReplicaInterface.small_market_expected_rank; no
large-market phase lemma is applied at m=1 or m=2.

5. BELOW-FIVE CERTIFICATE: DOES IT REALLY COVER m <= 10^80?

Yes. FiniteFiveCertificate has 21 tests: 20 interior horizons
1-2^(-1),...,1-2^(-20), then the endpoint t=1 with theta=1600.
There are 22 check nodes, including 0 and 1. All masses are nonnegative.

FiniteArrivalCertificate.kernel_concave_gap proves the needed concavity only
on intervals containing no test horizon in their interior. grid_domination
checks that gap condition for every horizon and every adjacent grid interval;
it does NOT incorrectly assume concavity across the positive-part kink.
The cases r=0, r=1, and r>1 are covered. value_le_certificate then applies the
pointwise domination to every nonnegative atom time and interchanges only
finite sums.

FiniteFiveCertificate.kernel_lower proves all 21*22 lower-kernel bounds using
exact rational Taylor-polynomial inequalities. endpoint_check verifies all
22 weighted inequalities. Interior price bounds use a proved logarithmic
allowance. log_ten_upper gives log 10 <= 2.302586. The endpoint price is exactly
1600+80*2.302586 = 11151293/6250.

Independent Python Fraction recomputation from the SOURCE TABLES verified:
- all 462 degree-eight Taylor witnesses for the kernel lower bounds;

  124982139465456527 / 25000000000000000
  = 4.99928557861826108 < 5;

This is an independent arithmetic check, not a replacement for Lean's proof.

PaperReplicaInterface.expectedPS_lt_five_of_arrival_bounds explicitly converts

PaperReplicaInterface.expectedPS_lt_five supplies actualExposure, the proven

finite market range and all positive integer quotas are genuinely covered.
