Verdict
-------
No concrete mathematical defect, vacuous premise, missing quota factor,
unpaid exceptional event, or surrogate-profile substitution was found in the
lower chain. The final theorem concerns the actual expected PS type rank for
m types, m*q agents, and independent uniform type rankings, for each fixed
positive integer q. Its conclusion is expectedPS(m,q)/logStar(m) -> 1.

The Lean argument proves the same final lower asymptotic as the manuscript by
a somewhat different finite estimate. It does not certify each displayed
intermediate constant or cutoff in the manuscript verbatim. This distinction
should be retained in any description of the formal verification.

1. Actual model and normalization
---------------------------------
ReplicaPS.Profile and profileSample use m*q agents with independent uniform
permutations of m types. liftPreference orders all q unit copies of a type
consecutively. favorite_type proves that the favorite remaining copy belongs
to the favorite remaining type. Thus deterministic internal copy transitions
do not change agents' type choices.

ReplicaPS.allocation aggregates the concrete PS allocations over copies.
allocation_agent_sum proves mass one per agent; allocation_type_sum proves
capacity q per type. agentRank uses type rank, averageRank averages over m*q
agents, and expectedPS averages over the original profile sample. These are
not defined in terms of the desired lower bound or an artificial raw process.

The raw contribution rawBandCost is weightedRawSum/m. Consequently one band
earns approximately q raw-cost units. ReplicaRawCostRank.rawCost_rank_reduction
converts raw cost to average rank with a factor alpha*q. The final q cancels
only after this conversion. No factor q is omitted from consumption or rank.

2. Exact original-profile/raw-label coupling
--------------------------------------------
ReplicaLaw.fresh_answer_probability derives conditional uniformity from the
exact finite path fiber via pathFiber_eq_compatibleProfiles and the admissible
fresh query's answerFiber cardinalities. Conditional uniformity is proved,
not postulated as independence of accepted answers.

ReplicaRawStep.rawWeight_sum, raw_label_uniform, profile_marginal,
accepted_weighted_expectation, and rejected_weighted_expectation establish
the finite raw-label transition. acceptedFiber_nonempty is proved before a
representative is chosen. acceptedFiber_eq_next_pathFiber identifies the
accepted fiber with the concrete next copy history.

ReplicaRawProcess.nextQueryDepth_fiber and State.normalize_fiber show that
skipping deterministic internal copy events preserves the posterior.
State.child_fiber proves that choosing a representative does not change the
conditional law. State.step_posterior_expectation and
State.evolve_posterior_expectation then establish the invariant for arbitrary
profile statistics. raw_prefix_profile_marginal returns exactly the original
profile average for every finite IID raw prefix, including rank and failure
indicators. No independence between the raw good event and the profile good
event is used.

ReplicaRawClock.clock_on_fiber/active_on_fiber and monotonicity connect the
reference state to every compatible actual profile. The finite terminal
depth (m*q)^2 reaches clock one, after which raw labels contribute zero
remaining-time weight. UniformRawPrefixes proves the prefix and restart
identities for the newest-first tuple convention; there is no temporal
orientation discrepancy.

3. Survivor counts and the phase-boundary draw
---------------------------------------------
ReplicaSurvivorEntries.fresh_agent_not_mem and survivorAgents_nodup show that
fresh entries into a set which survives are made by distinct agents.
entryCount_le_agents therefore bounds the count by m*q. Normalization adds
no fresh entries. entryCount_child/entryCount_evolve_survivor and
raw_count_survivor_eq_entries transfer this to raw labels.

The potentially delicate endpoint is handled explicitly:
fresh_entryCount_succ_le_agents and raw_count_before_step_le_agents include
the final label whose subsequent normalization may already advance beyond a
round or exhaust the set. The raw-prefix rank bridge does not incorrectly
apply a post-transition survivor bound to that final draw.

ReplicaRawRankBridge.roundDuration_tail,
weightedRawSum_eq_round_counts, and rawBandCost_eq_round_counts identify the
weighted raw contribution with the actual round-duration/count expression
for every profile in the final fiber. rawRoundCount counts using normalized
pre-draw depth. rawRoundCount_eq_length_of_depth and
RawPrefixGood.rawRoundCount_bound justify its deterministic upper bound.

4. Untouched capacity and predictable consumption
--------------------------------------------------
ReplicaUntouched.entryCount_evolve_le_raw, untouched_entryCount_zero,
no_entry_no_previous_choice, and no_entry_consumption_zero show that a type
absent from the raw labels has never been consumed. untouched_residual_eq_quota
therefore proves its actual residual capacity is q. Deterministic copy
normalization cannot consume a supposedly untouched type unnoticed.

ReplicaRawBands.remainingWeight uses 1 minus the normalized clock BEFORE
the next label. expected_weightedRawHits holds for arbitrary weights depending
on the past state. expected_hits_per_type divides by the fixed set's size to
obtain exactly expected rawBandCost. When the set is the untouched set at a
prefix, it is fixed during the conditional future-block average; future IID
labels are not conditioned on a later high-probability event.

ReplicaConsumptionLower.consumption_le_full_weighted_entries bounds actual
consumption by arrivals weighted with their remaining time. Removing negative
exit contributions has the safe inequality direction.
consumption_at_clock_le_entries and weightedEntries_child/step_le/evolve_le
transfer this bound through normalization and rejection draws.
consumed_set_le_raw_hits and band_quota_le_hits_and_residual give the exact
capacity-minus-residual inequality. expected_bandCost_lower averages it
without any conditioning on a good event.

5. Coupon and survivor probability bounds
-----------------------------------------
CouponLowerBound proves the exact unseen mean m*(1-1/m)^k, pair mean, negative
pair covariance, and variance at most the mean for m>=2. Chebyshev then gives
the bad-stock probability 4/mean; positivity of the mean is discharged.
The exponential lower estimate is deliberately loose, m*exp(-2*k/m).

RawSubsetCounts gives the fixed-set lower-tail Chernoff bound under IID raw
labels. RawSurvivorBound.survivor_card_tail takes a union over fixed subsets,
then applies the pathwise survivor count to the endogenous actual active set.
Its mean hypothesis is required explicitly. total_residual_le_active_quota
and expected_residual_le retain the full rare-event cost q*m*probability.

ReplicaBandLower.expected_band_lower_by_residual uses a pathwise bad-stock
correction q*indicator and residual/(mean/2). On the bad event, nonnegativity
of raw cost justifies the correction. Both terms are averaged before any
event is discarded. expected_band_lower substitutes the residual estimate;
expected_cost_ge_band_sum telescopes adjacent blocks using proved restart
identities, monotone cutoffs, and the mean condition for EVERY used band.
Omitting the raw contribution before cutoff zero is justified by positivity.

6. Static profile rank concentration and the rank bridge
---------------------------------------------------------
UniformLowerRank.expected_firstRank is exactly (m+1)/(s+1). Its scaled-rank
mean is at least 1-1/(s+1). A loose uniform second-moment bound
16*(1+exp(1)) and a lower-tail exponential bound yield concentration across
the original independent agent rankings.

ReplicaLowerRank.UniformLowerRankGood is simultaneous over a fixed family of
sets with sizes between lo and hi. uniformLowerRankGood_failure_probability
keeps the family factor. meanRank_lower_with_cutoff extends control to smaller
sets by enlarging to lo, under the explicit hypotheses lo<=m and lo<=hi.
Larger sets are handled separately by the coarse raw-count bound.

RawPrefixGood.CountsGood quantifies over all finite prefixes and the fixed
set family, conditional only on the deterministic mean inequality. Its
failure bound retains the (N+1)*family.card union factor. CountsGood.tail/full
ensure it applies at the relevant actual prefix. The endpoint-sensitive
rawRoundCount_bound uses the pre-step count bound described above.

ReplicaRawCostRank.rawCost_rank_reduction combines these statements for each
profile in the final fiber. The horizon condition controls sets below lo;
the coarse all-subsets event controls sets above hi; the fine event controls
the middle range. Summed round durations equal one. The resulting large-set
overhead is K*4*(m*q)/hi, with no missing time or agent normalization.

expectedPS_lower_from_rawCost retains BOTH failure costs: the raw-count
failure and the static original-profile rank failure, each multiplied by
the deterministic worst-case cost K*N/m. The exact profile marginal is used
separately on averageRank and on the rank-failure indicator. It never assumes
that good-event conditioning preserves independence.

FiniteLowerBound.expectedPS_ge_explicit_bands combines the band sum, horizon
monotonicity, pathwise rank bridge, and both explicit failure probabilities.
All finite errors are still present at this interface.

7. Parameters, cutoffs, accumulated errors, and final limit
---------------------------------------------------------
LowerBoundEntropy.small_family_card_le_rate and exists_small_family_rate
prove the required family cardinality bounds by an exponential generating
function. The entropy rate (B+1)*exp(-B) can be chosen arbitrarily small.

FixedQuotaParameters.parameters_exist explicitly constructs the complete
parameter record. It uses L=ceil(2/eta), a=1-eta/2,
theta=eta/(4*C), C=16*(1+exp(1)), then chooses positive concentration slack c,
a sufficiently small family entropy rate, and D=64*q/eta+4. The parameter
premises are feasible; no desired rank or asymptotic conclusion is stored
as an assumed field.

LowerBoundCutoffs.mean_at_rawCutoff_ge, next_rawCutoff_ge,
survivorCutoff_mean, and survivorCutoff_le account for floors and ceilings.
The raw cutoff is floor(m*x/2), consistent with the looser unseen-mean bound.
LowerBoundBandErrors.bandLowerValue_ge gives per-band lower contribution
q - 32*q^2/D - bandError(m,q); the q^2 loss is retained.

LowerBoundSchedule.completedBands conservatively subtracts the terminal
crossing band. completed_band_scale_bound keeps every used endpoint below
log(m)/10. cutoffs_mono, schedule_mean, rawCutoff_le_horizon, and
horizon_rank_cutoff discharge every finite-interface condition. The chosen
horizon is floor(m^2/L), rather than the shorter manuscript horizon.

LowerBoundErrors.exceptionalBound_le keeps both prefix-union factors and
the rank family factor. LowerBoundMarket.weighted_exceptionalBound_le pays
the worst-case raw cost and bounds the result by
K*(2*m^3+3*m)*exp(-c*m). polynomial_error_tendsto proves its decay.

FixedQuotaLowerBound.eventually_lower_with_loss first fixes q and eta, then
makes bandError <= eta*q/2. Together with the deterministic band loss this
gives EVERY band at least (1-eta)*q before summation over the growing number
of bands. It does not discard a per-band o(1) without controlling the band
count. The separate exceptional-event cost is bounded by one and enters the
additive constant. The high-set overhead is also a fixed additive constant.

LowerBoundBands and LowerBoundSchedule.logStar_le_completedBands_add give
J <= logStar(m) <= J+C with C depending only on the fixed parameters.
StandardLogStar.logStar is the literal least number of natural-log iterations
needed to reach at most one; logStar_tendsto_atTop is proved, not assumed.

expectedPS_div_logStar_tendsto_one proves the lower limiting bound using
eventually_lower_with_loss and sends eta to zero by an explicit choice for
each target b<1. It obtains the upper limiting bound from the independent
finite upper theorem expectedPS <= logStar+17/5. The only final assumptions
are fixed q:Nat and 0<q. eventually_expectedPS_ge is a direct useful corollary.

8. Scope and changes
---------------------
No mathematical fix is indicated by this audit, and no source was changed.
No sorry, admit, custom axiom, unsafe declaration, implemented_by attribute,
or sorryAx occurrence was found in the lower-specific source files searched.
This source scan supplements, and does not substitute for, the separate
kernel build and transitive axiom audit being run by the root agent.

The manuscript lower proof uses x0=8q, cutoffs floor(m*x), B=32q/eta,
a horizon floor(m*log(m)/10), and exact mean-normalized rank concentration
over all small sets. Lean instead uses x0=1, floor(m*x/2), D=64q/eta+4,
fixed lower rank cutoff L, and horizon floor(m^2/L). Both prove the same
fixed-positive-integer-quota asymptotic. Do not describe the latter as a
verbatim verification of the former's intermediate inequalities/constants.

The fixed-quota chain alone does not claim a limit uniformly over arbitrary
growing quotas. Any bounded-quota or high-quota result requires its own
separately proved interface. This is correct theorem scope, not a defect.

One harmless API detail: ReplicaBandLower.bandLowerValue has an unused ending
argument. The actual ending cutoff remains in the mandatory mean hypothesis
of expected_band_lower and expected_cost_ge_band_sum, so this does not hide a
missing assumption. Removing the unused argument would be cosmetic API churn
and is not recommended under the user's surgical-change instruction.

An independent delegated read of the parameter/cutoff/error/schedule chain
and its manuscript correspondence reached the same conclusions. It also
found no concrete defect in the manuscript's lower-bound second moment,
entropy union, coefficient-replacement error, untouched-stock variance,
conditional expectation, or scale count.
