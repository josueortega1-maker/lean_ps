Compatibility adapter:
The pinned Lean runtime locates its own executable by readlink on
/proc/<getpid>/exe. This container permits the same process's executable
through /proc/self/exe but denies the numeric spelling (errno 13).
own-process-path.c compiles into own-process-path.so and maps only the exact
current process's numeric executable path to /proc/self/exe in readlink.
All other readlink calls pass unchanged to the original libc function.
No Lean executable, kernel, dependency source, proof source, or permission
setting is modified by this adapter. No other process is accessed.
